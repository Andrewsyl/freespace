## Short Description

Find and book trusted parking spaces nearby.

## Full Description

FreeSpace helps drivers find and reserve parking spaces with clear arrival details, upfront pricing, and live booking status.

Use FreeSpace to:

- search for available parking near your destination
- book and pay securely in advance
- view arrival details and access instructions after booking
- manage upcoming, active, and past bookings
- save your vehicle details for faster checkout

For hosts, FreeSpace makes it easy to list a space, manage availability, and handle bookings in one place.
